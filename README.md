# Konrad's Journey

An interactive exploration of cultural differences and similarities between Germany and Turkey.

## About the Project

**Konrad's Journey** was created as part of an Intercultural Communication project at DHBW Stuttgart.

The project explores how cultural expectations influence everyday interactions. Instead of presenting the results as a traditional report, we developed a multimedia one-page website combining short stories, cultural comparisons, illustrations and audio content.

The fictional character Konrad guides the reader through situations in Germany and Turkey. Each situation demonstrates how behavior that appears natural in one cultural context may be understood differently in another.

## Topics

The project examines topics such as:

- approaching and speaking to strangers
- greetings and physical gestures
- hospitality and the role of guests
- family relationships
- age and social hierarchy
- punctuality and perceptions of time
- direct and indirect communication
- high-context and low-context communication
- personal space and non-verbal behavior
- structure, flexibility and social expectations

The objective is not to define either culture through rigid stereotypes. Instead, the project illustrates tendencies and encourages readers to consider the cultural context behind people's behavior.

## Why Germany and Turkey?

Germany and Turkey provide an interesting comparison because their societies differ in several communicative and social conventions while also being closely connected through migration, business, education and personal relationships.

The comparison allowed us to examine how values such as directness, hospitality, hierarchy, punctuality and community can influence everyday communication.

## Project Format

The final result is an interactive multimedia one-page website containing:

- narrative blog entries
- German–Turkish cultural comparisons
- custom pictograms and visual elements
- audio content
- practical everyday situations
- a consistent visual storytelling concept

The website was designed to make the subject more approachable and memorable than a conventional written report.

## What I Learned

The project strengthened my understanding of:

- intercultural communication
- German and Turkish cultural conventions
- high-context and low-context communication
- cultural self-reflection
- multimedia storytelling
- visual consistency
- responsible and transparent use of AI
- transforming academic content into an accessible digital format

One of the central lessons was that successful intercultural communication requires more than translating words. It also requires understanding expectations, relationships, social roles and the context in which communication takes place.
